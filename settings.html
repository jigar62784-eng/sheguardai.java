Legal & Security Compliance

Privacy Commitment

SheGuard does not monetize, sell, or profile your personal data. All GPS coordinates broadcasted to the Public Safety Map are intentionally fuzzed by approximately 500 meters to ensure home and workplace anonymity. Exact coordinates are shared strictly with mutually consented family circle members and verified emergency responders during active SOS distress calls.

Terms of Service

SheGuard is designed as an auxiliary safety and reporting system. In an active, life-threatening emergency, users should always dial official emergency services (112 or local police) directly whenever physically safe to do so.

Danger Zone

Permanently delete your user account, delete your personal profile, and revoke all family links from the database.
